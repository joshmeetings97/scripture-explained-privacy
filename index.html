Scripture Explained Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Scripture Explained YouTube channel.

What this app is

Scripture Explained Uploader is a small automation used by one person, the owner of the Scripture Explained YouTube channel, to run that channel. It is not a public service and has no other users: only the channel owner signs in to it, with the Google account that owns the channel.

What it does

  • Prepares one Bible-teaching video each week (plus short clips) and uploads them to the Scripture Explained channel, including titles, descriptions and thumbnails.
  • Organizes the channel's videos into playlists, and keeps the channel's own description and banner up to date.
  • Reads comments that viewers leave on the channel's videos and posts replies to questions and challenges, with scripture and encouragement. For the first weeks, the owner approves each reply before it is posted.

Why it asks for access to the YouTube account, and what for

Permission requestedWhy it is needed
Upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload)To publish the channel's weekly videos and short clips.
Manage your YouTube account (youtube)To set video thumbnails, create and fill the channel's playlists, and update the channel's own description and banner.
View, edit and delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions (youtube.force-ssl)To read the comments on the channel's own videos and post replies to them, and to read a video's captions so replies are accurate.

The app uses this access only for the channel owner's own channel, and only for the purposes above. It does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone for any other purpose.
