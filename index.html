Scripture Explained Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Scripture Explained Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Scripture Explained YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

The tool signs in to the channel owner's Google account through Google's OAuth sign-in. It uses that access only to upload videos, set thumbnails, update channel and video details, and manage playlists on the Scripture Explained channel.

What it collects

It does not collect, sell, share or store personal information about any person. The only credential it keeps is the owner's sign-in token, which is stored privately by the owner and used only by this tool.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
