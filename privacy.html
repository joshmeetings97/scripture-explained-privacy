Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Scripture Explained Uploader (“the app”) is a private tool used by the owner of the Scripture Explained YouTube channel to publish that channel's videos and respond to its comments. It is not offered to the public, and the channel owner is its only user.

Google user data the app accesses

The channel owner signs in with Google. The app then accesses the owner's YouTube channel to upload videos and thumbnails, manage the channel's playlists and description, read the captions of the channel's videos, and read and reply to comments on the channel's videos. The app requests the following scopes: youtube.upload, youtube and youtube.force-ssl.

Viewers' comments

To reply to comments, the app reads the public comments that viewers have posted on the channel's videos, including the commenter's public display name and the comment text. The text of a comment, along with the video's title, description and transcript, is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to draft a reply. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than drafting that reply. Draft replies, with the commenter's display name and comment, are held in a private GitHub repository for the channel owner to review. The app keeps a list of the comment IDs it has already handled so it does not respond twice.

What is stored, and where

  • The channel owner's Google sign-in token, kept in private, encrypted storage and used only by the app.
  • Comment IDs, draft replies and video transcripts, kept in a private repository accessible only to the channel owner.

The app does not collect or store any other personal information about viewers.

Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data, does not share it with third parties for their own use, and does not use it for advertising. The only service that receives comment text is Anthropic's API, as described above, to provide the reply feature. People do not read Google user data other than the channel owner reviewing the replies the app drafts.

Your choices

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. A viewer who wants a comment removed from the app's records can write to the address below, and it will be deleted.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
